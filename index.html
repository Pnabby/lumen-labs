A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Is STEWARD V.A. available to buy?+

STEWARD is currently in product refinement and early pilot development. Commercial availability and pricing have not been announced. Contact us to discuss pilot participation or future availability.

Does it need an internet connection?+

Selected functions are designed to work locally without continuous internet connectivity. The availability and performance of individual features are being evaluated during development and pilot testing.

Who is STEWARD being designed for?+

STEWARD is being designed with and for people who are blind or have low vision, with a focus on everyday tasks and African environments. User feedback is central to its development.

How can my organization get involved?+

We welcome conversations about accessibility research, pilot participation, manufacturing, distribution, investment and other partnerships. Send an enquiry above or email lumenlabs@gmail.com.
